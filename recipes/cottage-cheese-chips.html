Cottage Cheese Chips

Ingredients

  • 1 (16-ounce) container of cottage cheese
  • 1 tablespoon everything bagel seasoning

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 400 degrees F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Scoop tablespoon-size portions of cottage cheese on the baking sheet, 2 inches apart. Use the back of a spatula to slightly flatten each portion.
  3. Sprinkle with everything bagel seasoning, if desired.
  4. Bake until golden brown and crisp, about 30 minutes. Let cool for 5 minutes before enjoying.
